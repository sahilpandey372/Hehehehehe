There are people who come into your life quietly — and then there are people like you, Ishu. The ones who rearrange everything, without even trying.

The reasons I love you

01 🌙

You carry yourself with a quiet strength that takes my breath away. Not the loud, forceful kind — but the kind that bends without breaking, that stands tall even on the hardest days. Watching you move through the world with such grace makes me believe in people more than I ever thought I could.

02 💫

The way you talk to me — like I'm the only person in the room, like I matter, like I am enough — is something I never knew I needed until I had it. You make me feel like the luckiest person alive, not because of grand gestures, but because of the warmth in your voice when you say my name.

03 ⚡

When the world gets messy — and it always does — you face it with a clarity that leaves me in awe. You don't crumble, you don't panic. You look a difficult moment straight in the eye and handle it with such poise that I find myself thinking: she is, without a doubt, the strongest person I know.

04 ✨

Your smile — oh, Ishu. Your smile is the kind of thing poets spend their whole lives trying to describe. And your laugh? It is the most honest, beautiful sound I have ever heard. It is my favorite thing in the world. It always will be.

✦

Forever yours ♡

written somewhere between the stars, just for you
